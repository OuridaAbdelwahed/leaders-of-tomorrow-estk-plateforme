INTERNAL REGULATIONS OF THE LEADERS OF TOMORROW CLUB
Leaders of Tomorrow Club

Version: 1.0
Academic Year: 2026–2027
Effective Date: 29 July 2026
Last Updated: 29 July 2026

These Internal Regulations are the official operating reference for every Club member. They define membership, governance, participation, conduct, communications, partnerships, finance, and discipline. Every member must comply from the date their membership is approved.

1. Nature and mission of the Club

Leaders of Tomorrow is a student club affiliated with its educational institution. It develops leadership, innovation, technology, entrepreneurship, volunteerism, and community-service skills.

2. Governing principles

The Club is guided by leadership, responsibility, respect, integrity, teamwork, transparency, creativity, volunteering, equality, and community service.

3. Reputation

Members must protect the reputation of the Club and the institution on and off campus.

4. Club interest

The legitimate interest of the Club takes priority over personal or factional interests in Club decisions.

5. Official application channel

Applications are accepted only through the Club’s official electronic platform.

6. Membership application requirements

Applicants must create one account, complete required information, provide a recent photograph and CV where requested, select and pay a membership contribution of at least 50 MAD, and electronically accept these Regulations and the related mandatory policies.

7. Activation of membership

Membership becomes active only after application review, document verification, contribution confirmation, and approval by the Executive Board or its delegate.

8. Digital membership card

Every approved member receives a verified digital membership card containing a QR code. It is the official proof of membership.

9. Personal use only

Using another member’s account, card, badge, ticket, or identity is prohibited in all circumstances.

10. Renewal

Membership is renewed under conditions published by the Executive Board.

11. Official platform

The platform is the official reference for membership, renewal, cards, invitations, notices, certificates, news, activities, profiles, and participation history.

12. Platform services

Members must use platform services lawfully, accurately, and only for their intended Club purposes.

13. Credential confidentiality

Every member must protect account credentials and immediately report suspected compromise.

14. One-account rule

Each person may maintain only one Club account. Duplicate or deceptive accounts may be suspended.

15. Activity invitations

Official activity invitations are issued through the platform or other authorized Club channels.

16. Attendance confirmation

Confirming attendance through the platform is a formal commitment to attend.

17. Excused absence

Absence after confirmation is permitted for an academic session, official examination, illness, compelling family circumstance, or another reason accepted by the Executive Board.

18. Notice of absence

Members must inform the responsible organizers as soon as they know they cannot attend.

19. Repeated absence

Repeated unexplained absence may affect the member’s evaluation, invitations, committee participation, leadership eligibility, and certificate eligibility.

20. Temporary restriction

The Executive Board may temporarily restrict activities or responsibilities after repeated unexplained absence or comparable non-performance.

21. Punctuality

All participants must arrive at the announced time and comply with organizer and venue instructions.

22. Member rights

Members may participate in eligible activities and training, receive certificates when requirements are met, submit proposals, and apply for responsibilities under applicable selection rules.

23. Member duties

Members must respect these Regulations and all people, complete accepted tasks, protect Club property and confidential information, and represent the Club professionally.

24. Assignment of responsibility

Responsibilities are assigned according to competence, commitment, initiative, fairness, and operational need.

25. Leadership development

The Executive Board may delegate responsibilities to help members develop leadership skills while maintaining appropriate supervision.

26. Team supervision

Every designated lead is responsible for following up with their team and reporting material risks or delays.

27. Leaving a responsibility

A member may not abandon an accepted responsibility without reasonable prior notice and a proper handover.

28. Respect and equality

Members must treat one another with dignity and without unlawful discrimination.

29. Harassment and violence

Harassment, violence, humiliation, bullying, intimidation, or conduct that violates another person’s dignity is prohibited.

30. Professional conduct

Members must communicate and behave professionally during every physical or online Club activity.

31. Harmful publications

Publishing false, confidential, abusive, or deliberately harmful content about the Club, institution, members, partners, or participants is prohibited.

32. Personal disputes

Personal disagreements must not disrupt Club activities, teamwork, professional relationships, or decision-making.

33. Separation of interests

Members must separate personal relationships from Club work and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

34. Mediation

Where a dispute affects the Club, the Executive Board or a designated committee may mediate and adopt an appropriate, fair decision.

35. Official communications

The platform and authorized Club channels are the official sources for announcements and notices.

36. Public representation

No member may issue an official statement, negotiate, sign, or represent the Club externally without written authorization.

37. Visual identity

Members must preserve the Club’s name, logo, brand assets, and graphic standards and may use them only as authorized.

38. Partnerships

The Club may build mission-aligned relationships with educational institutions, associations, companies, public and private bodies, and sponsors.

39. External negotiations

No member may negotiate on behalf of the Club with an external party without a formal assignment.

40. Partner treatment

Members must welcome partners and sponsors respectfully and represent the Club to a high professional standard.

41. Financial transparency

Club financial resources must be managed transparently, accurately, and within available means and approved controls.

42. Use of funds

Membership contributions, donations, and sponsorship funds may be used only for legitimate Club activities, operations, and mission-related purposes.

43. Reporting

The Executive Board may prepare periodic financial reports and supporting records when appropriate.

44. Progressive discipline

Depending on severity and circumstances, measures may include oral guidance, written warning, restriction of privileges, suspension, or termination of membership.

45. Right to respond

Before a disciplinary decision, the member should normally receive notice and a reasonable opportunity to respond, except for urgent safety or serious-misconduct measures.

46. Serious violations

Serious violations include impersonation, forged information or documents, deliberate reputational harm, unauthorized disclosure, verbal or physical assault, fraud, security abuse, and using the Club’s identity for personal gain.

47. Academic priority

Study and academic achievement remain a priority. Activities should be organized so they do not unreasonably harm academic progress.

48. Amendments

These Regulations may be amended by approval of two thirds of the Executive Board. Members must be notified of material amendments.

49. Entry into force

These Regulations enter into force upon approval by the Executive Board and on the effective date published above.

50. Member undertaking

Every approved member acknowledges and agrees to comply with these Internal Regulations throughout their membership. Electronic acceptance through the platform records this undertaking, subject to applicable law.

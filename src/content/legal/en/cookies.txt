1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how the Leaders of Tomorrow Platform ("Platform", "Website", "Club", "we", "our", or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience, protect our services, secure event registrations, and manage memberships.

By using our Platform, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this Policy, unless you choose to disable them through your browser settings where possible.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser when you visit a website.

Cookies help websites:

Remember your preferences
Keep you logged in
Improve security
Detect fraudulent activity
Remember language settings
Improve performance
Analyze website usage

Cookies cannot execute programs or install software on your device.

5. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the operation of the Platform and cannot be disabled through the Platform itself.

They are used to:

Load pages correctly
Maintain secure sessions
Process forms
Prevent CSRF attacks
Protect against unauthorized access
Support Cloudflare security services
Enable secure navigation

Without these cookies, many Platform features will not function properly.

6. Authentication Cookies

Authentication cookies help verify your identity after you sign in.

These cookies may be used to:

Keep you signed in during your session
Recognize your verified account
Prevent repeated authentication requests
Protect authenticated pages

Authentication cookies are encrypted and are not intended to store your password in plain text.

7. Security Cookies

Security cookies help us protect the Platform against abuse and malicious activity.

These cookies may support:

Email OTP verification
Device verification
Session integrity
Account protection
Fraud detection
Login monitoring
Abuse prevention

Security cookies may also be used alongside browser fingerprinting and rate limiting to detect suspicious activity.

8. Preference Cookies

Preference cookies remember choices you make to improve your experience, including:

Preferred language (Arabic, French, or English)
Theme preferences (where available)
Accessibility settings
User interface preferences

These cookies help provide a more personalized experience.

9. Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the Platform.

We may use aggregated analytics information to measure:

Number of visitors
Most visited pages
Event registrations
Ticket usage
Device types
Browser compatibility
Performance metrics

Analytics information is used to improve the Platform and is not used to identify individual users where not necessary.

10. Event Registration Cookies

To ensure fair access to events, cookies may be used to:

Maintain registration sessions
Prevent duplicate submissions
Support one-ticket-per-user enforcement
Store temporary registration information
Improve event registration reliability

These cookies work together with other security measures such as email verification and browser fingerprint verification.

11. Membership Cookies

Membership cookies may be used to:

Recognize approved members
Display member-specific content
Load digital badges
Access member-only areas
Maintain secure membership sessions

These cookies expire according to the Platform's security settings or upon logout.

12. AI Feature Cookies

Where AI-powered services are available, cookies may be used to:

Maintain AI conversation sessions
Store temporary interaction preferences
Improve response continuity
Prevent abuse of AI services

These cookies are not used to make automated decisions about membership or event eligibility without human oversight.

13. Third-Party Cookies

Some features of the Platform rely on trusted third-party services.

These services may place cookies necessary to provide functionality such as:

Secure payment processing (for memberships or optional donations)
Email delivery
Embedded maps
Video content
Analytics
Cloud infrastructure

The use of these cookies is governed by the privacy policies of the respective service providers.

14. Cloudflare Cookies

The Platform uses Cloudflare services to improve security, reliability, and performance.

Cloudflare may place cookies that help:

Identify trusted visitors
Distinguish legitimate users from automated traffic
Protect against Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks
Support Web Application Firewall (WAF) protections
Validate Cloudflare Turnstile challenges
Maintain secure sessions

These cookies are used solely for security and performance purposes.

15. Cookie Retention

Cookies may remain on your device for different periods depending on their purpose.

Session Cookies are automatically deleted when you close your browser.
Persistent Cookies remain for a limited period or until manually deleted.

Retention periods are determined based on security requirements, operational needs, and applicable legal obligations.

16. Managing Cookies

View stored cookies
Delete cookies
Block cookies
Block third-party cookies
Configure automatic deletion
Receive notifications before cookies are stored

Instructions vary depending on your browser.

17. Disabling Cookies

You may disable certain cookies through your browser settings.

Please note that disabling essential cookies may result in:

Event registration failures
Ticket verification issues
Loss of language preferences
Reduced security protections

The Platform is not responsible for functionality issues caused by disabling required cookies.

Club means Leaders of Tomorrow.
Platform means the official Leaders of Tomorrow website and digital services.
Event means any conference, workshop, seminar, competition, training, meeting, sports activity, volunteer activity, networking session, or other program organized or supported by the Club.
Participant means an individual registered for an event.
Ticket means a digital authorization issued through the Platform for attendance at an event.

Every ticket contains a unique QR Code.

At event entry, organizers may scan the QR Code to verify:

1. Purpose

This Membership Agreement establishes the relationship between the Leaders of Tomorrow Club ("the Club") and every approved member.

It defines the rights, responsibilities, obligations, and privileges associated with Club membership and ensures a transparent, fair, and professional governance framework.

Membership in the Club is voluntary and subject to compliance with this Agreement, the Club's Internal Regulations, Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Code of Conduct.

2. Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement:

Club means Leaders of Tomorrow.
Platform means the official Leaders of Tomorrow digital platform.
Member means an individual whose membership application has been approved.
Executive Board means the governing body responsible for Club administration.
Committee means a working group established by the Club.
Digital Badge means the official electronic identification issued to approved members.
Membership Contribution means the annual financial contribution required for membership.
3. Eligibility

Any individual may apply for membership provided that they:

Meet the eligibility requirements announced by the Club.
Provide accurate and truthful information.
Complete the online application through the official Platform.
Accept all Club policies.
Pay the applicable membership contribution.
Demonstrate respectful and ethical conduct.

Submission of an application does not guarantee acceptance.

4. Membership Categories

The Club may establish one or more membership categories, including:

4.1 Regular Member

A standard approved member entitled to participate in Club activities.

4.2 Executive Board Member

A member elected or appointed to manage the Club.

4.3 Committee Member

A member assigned to one or more specialized committees.

4.4 Honorary Member

An individual recognized by the Club for exceptional service or contribution.

4.5 Alumni Member

A former student or former active member who remains associated with the Club.

The Executive Board may create additional categories as needed.

5. Membership Application

Membership applications must be submitted exclusively through the official Platform.

Applicants must provide:

Full legal name
Valid email address
Student ID (where applicable)
Academic institution
Academic program
Year of study
Recent profile photograph
Curriculum Vitae (CV)
Any additional information requested by the Club

Applicants must certify that all information provided is accurate and complete.

Providing false or misleading information may result in rejection or termination of membership.

6. Membership Approval Process

Membership applications are reviewed by the Executive Board or a designated Membership Committee.

The review may consider:

Completeness of the application
Eligibility requirements
Accuracy of submitted information
Compliance with Club policies
Available membership capacity

The Club reserves the right to:

Approve applications
Reject applications
Request additional information
Conduct interviews where appropriate

The Club is not obligated to provide detailed reasons for every decision.

7. Membership Contribution

Membership requires an annual contribution.

The minimum contribution is:

50 MAD

The Executive Board may establish higher contribution levels or optional support tiers.

Membership contributions are used to support:

Educational programs
Workshops
Community initiatives
Sports activities
Leadership development
Platform maintenance
Administrative expenses

Membership contributions are generally non-refundable except where required by applicable law or expressly approved by the Executive Board.

8. Rights of Members

Approved members have the right to:

Participate in Club activities.
Register for eligible events.
Access member-only content.
Receive a digital membership badge.
Receive event invitations.
Apply for committees.
Volunteer in Club projects.
Receive certificates where eligible.
Access training opportunities.
Submit ideas and proposals.
Receive official communications from the Club.
Be treated fairly and respectfully.

Rights may be limited or suspended in accordance with Club policies.

9. Responsibilities of Members

Members agree to:

Respect all Club policies.
Maintain accurate profile information.
Protect their account credentials.
Use the Platform responsibly.
Participate professionally in Club activities.
Respect Club property.
Respect fellow members, volunteers, speakers, partners, and organizers.
Maintain the reputation of the Club.
Report security concerns or policy violations.
Follow instructions provided during Club events.

Members are expected to conduct themselves with integrity both online and during in-person activities.

10. Attendance Requirements

Members are encouraged to participate actively in Club activities.

When a member confirms attendance for an event through the Platform, that confirmation constitutes a commitment to attend.

Unexcused absences may affect:

Future invitations
Committee participation
Leadership opportunities
Eligibility for certificates
Annual membership evaluations

Acceptable reasons for absence include:

Academic classes
Laboratory sessions
Examinations
Medical conditions
Family emergencies
Other exceptional circumstances approved by the Executive Board

Repeated unexplained absences may result in disciplinary measures.

11. Digital Membership Badge

Approved members receive a digital badge accessible through the Platform.

The badge may contain:

Member name
Membership number
Profile photograph
QR Code
Membership status
Expiration date
Club branding

The badge is:

Personal
Non-transferable
Digitally verified
Subject to revocation upon suspension or termination

Members must not:

Share badges
Modify badges
Duplicate badges
Allow another individual to use their badge

Improper use of a badge may result in immediate disciplinary action.

12. Member Profile

Members are responsible for maintaining accurate profile information.

The Platform allows members to manage information such as:

Contact details
Academic information
Committee assignments
Membership status
Participation history
Digital certificates
Digital badge

Members must promptly update inaccurate or outdated information.

13. Leadership Opportunities

Leadership positions are based on merit, commitment, professionalism, and the needs of the Club.

Eligibility may consider:

Attendance
Participation
Initiative
Teamwork
Communication
Respect for Club policies
Volunteer contributions

Appointment to leadership positions is not guaranteed and remains at the discretion of the Executive Board or the applicable selection process.

14. Committees

The Club may establish committees including, but not limited to:

Events Committee
Media & Communication Committee
Partnerships Committee
Sponsorship Committee
Technology Committee
Training & Education Committee
Logistics Committee
Community Service Committee

Committee assignments may change according to operational needs.

Committee members are expected to fulfill assigned responsibilities in a timely and professional manner.

15. Voting Rights

Where the Club's governance structure provides for member voting, eligible members may participate in elections or other voting processes in accordance with the Club's Constitution or Internal Regulations.

The Executive Board may establish eligibility requirements for participation in specific votes.

16. Code of Conduct

Members agree to:

Treat others with dignity and respect.
Avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or bullying.
Respect cultural and religious diversity.
Resolve disagreements professionally.
Keep personal conflicts separate from Club activities.
Protect confidential information.
Avoid actions that may damage the Club's reputation.

Violation of the Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary measures.

17. Membership Renewal

Membership is valid for the period established by the Club.

Renewal may require:

Updating personal information
Renewing acceptance of Club policies
Paying the annual membership contribution
Maintaining eligibility requirements

Failure to renew may result in the expiration of membership privileges.

18. Suspension

Membership may be temporarily suspended for reasons including:

Serious misconduct
Fraud
Misuse of Club resources
Repeated policy violations
Harassment
Abuse of digital badges
Sharing event tickets
Unauthorized access attempts
Actions that threaten the safety or reputation of the Club

During suspension, the member may temporarily lose access to:

Member-only services
Event registrations
Digital badge
Leadership roles
Committee assignments
19. Termination of Membership

The Club may terminate membership where appropriate, including for:

Fraudulent applications
Identity impersonation
Submission of false documents
Repeated disciplinary violations
Criminal misuse of the Platform
Sale or transfer of free event tickets
Unauthorized sharing of digital badges
Serious misconduct during Club activities
Persistent failure to comply with Club policies

Termination may result in permanent loss of membership privileges.

20. Resignation

Members may voluntarily resign at any time by submitting a request through the Platform or another official communication channel.

Resignation does not automatically entitle the member to a refund of membership contributions.

21. Privacy

Personal information collected during membership is processed in accordance with the Leaders of Tomorrow Privacy Policy.

Members acknowledge that certain information may be required for:

Membership verification
Badge generation
Event participation
Certificate issuance
Internal administration
22. Limitation of Liability

The Club is not responsible for:

Personal belongings lost during events.
Personal injuries resulting from failure to follow event instructions.
Technical interruptions beyond its reasonable control.
Third-party services integrated into the Platform.

Nothing in this Agreement limits liability where prohibited by applicable law.

23. Amendments

The Executive Board reserves the right to amend this Agreement when necessary.

Material changes will be communicated through the Platform before taking effect where reasonably practicable.

Continued membership after amendments constitutes acceptance of the revised Agreement.

24. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Morocco.

Any disputes arising from membership shall be subject to the competent courts of Morocco, unless otherwise required by applicable law.

25. Acceptance

By submitting a membership application, paying the required contribution, activating membership, or using member services through the Platform, you acknowledge that:

You have read this Membership Agreement.
You understand your rights and responsibilities.
You agree to comply with all Club policies.
You understand that failure to comply may result in suspension or termination of membership.

Electronic acceptance of this Agreement through the Leaders of Tomorrow Platform shall have the same legal effect as a handwritten signature, subject to applicable law.

1. Purpose

The Leaders of Tomorrow Club believes that strong partnerships with educational institutions, businesses, non-governmental organizations, government agencies, technology companies, media organizations, and community leaders are essential to achieving its mission.

This Sponsorship & Partnership Policy establishes a transparent, ethical, and professional framework governing all sponsorships and collaborative relationships entered into by the Club.

The objectives of this Policy are to:

Promote mutually beneficial collaborations.
Ensure transparency and accountability.
Protect the Club's independence.
Maintain public trust.
Support sustainable growth.
Prevent conflicts of interest.
Protect all parties involved.

3. Definitions

Sponsor

An individual or organization providing financial, material, technological, or promotional support.

Partner

An organization collaborating with the Club toward shared objectives without necessarily providing financial support.

Collaboration

Any joint activity, event, project, campaign, or initiative.

Executive Board

The governing body responsible for approving sponsorships and partnerships.

4. Guiding Principles

9. Partnership Approval Process

All partnerships require approval by the Executive Board.

The Board may evaluate:

Mission alignment
Reputation
Legal compliance
Educational value
Financial transparency
Community impact
Potential conflicts of interest

The Club reserves the right to reject any proposal inconsistent with its mission or values.

10. Rights of Sponsors

Sponsors may receive benefits agreed upon in writing, which may include:

Logo placement
Public recognition
Website listing
Event acknowledgments
Sponsor booth space
Speaking opportunities where appropriate
Networking opportunities
Recognition certificates

Benefits shall be proportionate to the sponsorship agreement.

11. Responsibilities of Sponsors

Sponsors agree to:

Respect the Club's independence.
Provide agreed support.
Comply with applicable laws.
Respect students and volunteers.
Avoid misleading advertising.
Use the Club's branding only as authorized.
Honor contractual commitments.

Sponsors must not interfere with the Club's governance or internal decision-making.

12. Rights of the Club

The Club reserves the right to:

Accept or reject sponsors.
Decline sponsorships inconsistent with its values.
Review sponsor conduct.
Remove sponsor recognition where justified.
Protect its reputation.
Control event organization.
Maintain editorial independence.
13. Responsibilities of the Club

The Club commits to:

Communicate honestly.
Use sponsorship funds responsibly.
Respect sponsorship agreements.
Recognize sponsors fairly.
Maintain accurate records.
Protect confidential information.
Promote successful collaborations.
14. Branding & Logo Usage

The Leaders of Tomorrow logo, name, colors, and visual identity remain the exclusive property of the Club.

Sponsors and partners may use Club branding only:

With prior written authorization.
For approved purposes.
During the agreed partnership period.

The Club may similarly use sponsor logos only with permission or as provided in the sponsorship agreement.

Neither party may modify the other's logo without written approval.

21. Ethical Standards

The Club will not knowingly enter into partnerships or sponsorships with organizations that:

Promote illegal activities.
Encourage violence.
Promote hate speech.
Engage in corruption.
Violate applicable laws.
Exploit students.
Discriminate unlawfully.
Damage the educational mission of the Club.

All collaborations should reflect the Club's commitment to ethical leadership and social responsibility.

22. Conflict of Interest

Executive Board members, committee members, and volunteers involved in sponsorship decisions must disclose any actual or potential conflicts of interest.

Individuals with a conflict of interest should not participate in decisions where their impartiality could reasonably be questioned.

23. Exclusivity

Unless expressly agreed in writing, sponsorships and partnerships are non-exclusive.

The Club reserves the right to collaborate with multiple organizations simultaneously, provided that such collaborations do not create material conflicts or breach existing agreements.

24. Confidentiality

The Club and its sponsors or partners shall respect the confidentiality of non-public information exchanged during the collaboration.

Confidential information may not be disclosed without authorization, except where required by law or by the applicable agreement.

25. Data Protection

Sponsors and partners shall not receive personal information about members, volunteers, or event participants unless:

The individual has provided appropriate consent;
Disclosure is necessary to deliver a jointly organized activity; or
Disclosure is otherwise required or permitted by applicable law.

Any sharing of personal information must comply with the Leaders of Tomorrow Privacy Policy and applicable data protection requirements.

26. Public Announcements

Public announcements regarding sponsorships or partnerships should be coordinated between the Club and the collaborating organization.

Neither party should issue misleading statements or imply endorsements beyond those expressly agreed.

27. Termination

The Club may suspend or terminate a sponsorship or partnership where:

Material terms of the agreement are breached.
The collaboration becomes inconsistent with the Club's mission or values.
The sponsor or partner engages in unlawful or unethical conduct.
Continued collaboration poses a significant reputational, operational, or legal risk.

Termination should, where reasonably possible, be communicated in writing.

28. Liability

Each party remains responsible for its own acts and omissions.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing or required by law, neither the Club nor its sponsors or partners shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising solely from the collaboration.

29. Policy Updates

The Club may revise this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in law, organizational needs, or best practices.

The latest version will be published on the Leaders of Tomorrow Platform.

Continued collaboration after updates take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy unless otherwise agreed in writing.

30. Contact Information

Organizations interested in sponsoring or partnering with the Leaders of Tomorrow Club may contact the Club through the official communication channels published on the Platform.

31. Acceptance

By entering into a sponsorship agreement, partnership agreement, memorandum of understanding, collaboration agreement, or any other formal arrangement with the Leaders of Tomorrow Club, the participating organization acknowledges that it has read, understood, and agrees to comply with this Sponsorship & Partnership Policy.

Electronic acceptance through the Leaders of Tomorrow Platform shall have the same effect as a handwritten signature, subject to applicable law.
